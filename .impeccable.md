## Design Context

### Users
- Self-hosted community admins and their members.
- They use the wallet + paid-room UI to add funds (top-up) and to unlock subscription access to paid rooms.

### Brand Personality
- Neutral, calm, “cool infrastructure.”
- Confident and dependable (payment actions feel precise and safe; no gimmicks).

### Aesthetic Direction
- Neutral cool-gray surfaces with a cyan primary accent (already defined in `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` / DaisyUI theme).
- Dark-first presentation (primary UI assumes dark theme styling while preserving usable light theme defaults).
- Avoid neon glow and obvious “AI aesthetic” fingerprints.

### Design Principles
- Balance-first hierarchy: show wallet balance clearly, then the next best action.
- Reduce uncertainty: make payment states explicit (pending, completed, failed).
- Keep the interface crisp and operational: minimal chrome, strong typography hierarchy, clear call-to-action buttons.
- Accessibility baseline: preserve DaisyUI + Tailwind focus-visible and contrast intent from `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`.
